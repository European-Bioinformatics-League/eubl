## Practice problems

- [Rosalind](https://rosalind.info/problems/list-view/): programming problems in bioinformatics, graded automatically. Good practice for the capture-the-flag round.
- [picoCTF](https://picoctf.org/): a free, beginner-friendly capture-the-flag platform for getting used to the format.
- [OverTheWire: Bandit](https://overthewire.org/wargames/bandit/): a game that teaches the Unix command line step by step.
- Past questions of the Liga Portuguesa de Bioinformática, on [lpb.pt](https://lpb.pt/).

## Courses and training

- [EMBL-EBI Training](https://www.ebi.ac.uk/training/): free online courses on databases, tools and data analysis.
- [ELIXIR TeSS](https://tess.elixir-europe.org/): a registry of bioinformatics training events and materials across Europe.
- [The Carpentries](https://carpentries.org/): open lessons on the shell, version control, Python and R.

## Open books

## Textbooks

### Bioinformatics

- Compeau, P., & Pevzner, P. A. (2018). *Bioinformatics Algorithms: An Active Learning Approach* (3rd ed.). Active Learning Publishers.
- Jones, N. C., & Pevzner, P. A. (2004). *An Introduction to Bioinformatics Algorithms*. MIT Press.
- Lesk, A. M. (2008). *Introduction to Bioinformatics* (3rd ed.). Oxford University Press.
- Mount, D. W. (2004). *Bioinformatics: Sequence and Genome Analysis* (2nd ed.). Cold Spring Harbor Laboratory Press.
- Rocha, M., & Ferreira, P. G. (2018). *Bioinformatics Algorithms: Design and Implementation in Python*. Academic Press.
