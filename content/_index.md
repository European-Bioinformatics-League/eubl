## Who can take part

Teams of two students enrolled in a higher-education institution, at any level from first-year undergraduate to doctoral studies. Team members may come from any discipline: biology, computer science, mathematics, medicine or anything else. Both must be over 18 when they register. The full eligibility criteria are in the [rules](/rules/).

## How teams progress

Each round selects the teams for the next one. The first round is an online set of questions; the second is a capture-the-flag contest of hands-on computational problems; the best teams then meet in person for a hackathon at the Bioinformatics Open Days 2027, where the winners are announced. [Read how each round works](/competition/).

## Where the league comes from

The European Bioinformatics League grows out of the [Liga Portuguesa de Bioinformática](https://lpb.pt/), which has run since 2022 and whose 2025 edition already drew teams from several European countries and beyond. [More about the league](/about/).
