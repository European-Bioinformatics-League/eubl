This notice is given under Articles 13 and 14 of the General Data Protection Regulation (Regulation (EU) 2016/679, "GDPR"). It was last updated on 15 November 2026.

## Who is responsible

The data controller is the organising committee of the European Bioinformatics League (EUBL), whose members are listed on the [About page](/about/). For any question about your data, or to exercise your rights, write to <contact@eubl.org>.

## What we collect

When you register a team, we collect the information requested in the registration form, which is typically:

- the name and e-mail address of each team member;
- the institution, degree programme and level of study of each member;
- the country of each member's institution;
- the information needed to check eligibility (for example, age over 18, or the date of a doctoral degree);
- the team name.

During the competition we also process each team's answers, submissions and scores, and, for finalists, the information needed to organise their participation in the final.

## Why we use it, and on what legal basis

| Purpose | Legal basis (GDPR, Art. 6(1)) |
|---|---|
| Registering teams and checking eligibility | (b) necessary for your participation in the competition, which you request by registering |
| Running the rounds, scoring and contacting teams about the competition | (b) necessary for your participation |
| Publishing results: team name, institution, country and score | (f) our legitimate interest in a transparent competition; you may object (see below) |
| Organising the final at the Bioinformatics Open Days 2027 | (b) necessary for your participation |
| Anonymous statistics about participation (for example, number of teams per country) | (f) our legitimate interest in reporting on and improving the competition |

We do not use your data for marketing, we do not sell it, and we do not use it for automated decision-making or profiling. Results are computed from your answers and submissions according to the published [rules](/rules/).

## Who receives it

- **Members of the organising and scientific committees**, who need it to run the competition.
- **Google**, which hosts the registration form and its responses (Google Forms). Google may process data outside the European Economic Area; Google LLC is certified under the EU–US Data Privacy Framework, which the European Commission has recognised as providing an adequate level of protection. See [Google's privacy policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy).
- **The providers of the platforms used for the online rounds**, which will be named on this website before each round.
- **The organisers of the Bioinformatics Open Days 2027** (University of Minho, Braga), for finalists only and only as needed to organise the final.

## How long we keep it

Registration data and competition records are kept until the end of the 2027 edition, and deleted no later than 30 June 2028. Published results (team name, institution, country and score) remain on this website as part of the record of the edition, unless you object.

## Your rights

You have the right to access your data, to have it corrected or erased, to restrict or object to its processing, and to receive it in a portable format. Erasing your data before the end of the competition means withdrawing your team. To exercise any of these rights, write to <contact@eubl.org>; we answer within one month.

You also have the right to lodge a complaint with a data protection supervisory authority, in particular in the EU country where you live or work. In Portugal, this is the [Comissão Nacional de Proteção de Dados (CNPD)](https://www.cnpd.pt/).

## This website

This website sets no cookies, uses no analytics, and loads no content from third parties. It is hosted on GitHub Pages, which may log visitors' IP addresses for security purposes; see the [GitHub General Privacy Statement](https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement). Following the link to the registration form takes you to Google, whose own privacy policy and cookies then apply.

## Changes to this notice

We may update this notice, for example when the platforms for the online rounds are chosen. The date of the last update is given at the top.
